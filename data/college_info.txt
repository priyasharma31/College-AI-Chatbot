NOIDA INTERNATIONAL UNIVERSITY (NIU) - COLLEGE INFORMATION

==================================================
UNIVERSITY OVERVIEW
==================================================

University Name:
Noida International University (NIU)

Location:
Noida International University is located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Campus:
NIU has a 75-acre campus.

International Community:
NIU has students from more than 60 countries.

Academic Structure:
NIU offers 75+ courses through 13 schools.

Facilities and Opportunities:
The university provides accommodation and transport facilities.
The university also offers international student exchange opportunities.

Medical Education:
Noida International Institute of Medical Sciences is associated with the university.

SCHOLARSHIPS
==================================================

NIU provides scholarship opportunities.

The university's school information mentions scholarships of up to 50% for meritorious students.

For the 2026-27 domestic course information, scholarships are applicable only to tuition fees for the first year for the courses where the note is specified.

A one-time refundable security fee of INR 10,000 is also specified on the relevant 2026-27 course information.

Students should verify the latest scholarship eligibility, conditions and applicable amount from the official NIU website before applying.
